# Private Group Desktop Test Steps

Use three signed-in browser accounts when you have a desktop: one Ruler, one Writer, and one Watcher. Keep the public local demo closed or in a separate browser profile during this check.

## 1. Ruler creates the group

Sign in at `/team`, create a small test group, and share only its displayed private group number. Confirm that the public writing page still opens without sign-in and does not show the group name, members, or canon.

## 2. Writer submits a decision

The Writer requests to join as **Writer**. The Ruler approves the request. The Writer then enters a short Canon Proposal manually, such as a location decision. Confirm it shows as **Pending** for the Writer and is not visible in the Watcher’s approved canon list.

## 3. Ruler approves and revises canon

The Ruler approves the proposal. Confirm that both Writer and Watcher can find it using Canon Finder. The Ruler selects **Revise with history**, changes one sentence, and saves. Confirm the current approved record changes and **View history** still shows the earlier wording and optional revision note.

## 4. Watcher boundary

The Watcher requests to join as **Watcher**. Confirm that the Watcher can search and read approved canon but cannot see a Canon Proposal form, pending review list, Ruler history, revision button, approval button, or reject button.

## 5. Privacy check

On every account, return to the public local demo. Confirm that no local chapter, Story Vault item, local PDF import, or browser-local note appeared in the private group canon unless someone explicitly typed a new Canon Proposal.
