# The Ren Protocol — Product Tour Video

## Purpose

Create a short, beginner-friendly product tour for the final version of **The Ren Protocol**. The video must use only safe example material and must not expose, claim to use, or reproduce the author’s private writing, Masterbook, Vault, or group records.

## Format

The proposed video is a vertical 9:16 mobile-first product tour of roughly 60–75 seconds. It uses an energetic game-codex visual language, calm English voiceover, large readable headings, and short scene transitions.

## Scene Sequence

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 sec | The Ren Protocol emblem over a dark indigo story-console interface. | “The Ren Protocol is your private story console for writing, worldbuilding, and keeping every idea in one place.” |
| 6–15 sec | Pocket Desk with five clear destinations: Desk, Write, World, Plan, Vault. | “Start at your Desk. From here, write the next scene, open your world, plan the story path, or capture a new idea.” |
| 15–25 sec | Write screen with a blank sample chapter, then Writer Pulse. | “Write chapters in private. Your progress and focused writing sessions stay in your browser.” |
| 25–37 sec | Masterbook Atlas transitions through character dossier, custom stats, Place Folio, and Relationship Web. | “Build a living world atlas: character dossiers, custom stats, locations, story rules, and relationships you choose yourself.” |
| 37–47 sec | One-lane Planner and quest/scene cards. | “Plan one lane at a time. Turn loose ideas into scenes, arcs, and clear next moves without getting lost in a giant board.” |
| 47–57 sec | Pocket Vault capture options: Text, File, Link, then Archive and review controls. | “Drop notes, files, and links into your private Vault. Nothing is organized, sent, or filed until you choose it.” |
| 57–67 sec | Player Backup window selects a sample character, downloads a file, then shows a restore preview. | “Need a focused backup? Save one character’s stats and story progress locally. Restoring always previews first and adds a separate copy.” |
| 67–75 sec | Return to Pocket Desk with a lock icon and final title. | “Your story stays under your control. The Ren Protocol: write the next move.” |

## Non-Negotiable Privacy Statements

The video must accurately say that the main author workspace is browser-local by default; AI help requires an explicit review/consent step; Player Backup downloads locally; and no local information is automatically sent, shared, or made canon.

## Visual Constraints

Use dark midnight-indigo, violet, warm parchment, and gold accents. Present readable, clean interface-inspired scenes with fictional placeholder text only. Do not display real personal names, actual chapters, uploaded files, email addresses, or claimed user reviews.
