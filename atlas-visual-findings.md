# Masterbook Atlas Visual Findings

The desktop and 375-pixel phone checks confirm that the new Masterbook opens as an Atlas Shelf rather than seven repeated record sections. The shelf keeps the category destinations visible at once: Character Codex, System Board, World Map, Lore Glossary, Faction Hall, Artifact Cabinet, and Quest Board. On a phone, the category destinations remain two large tap targets per row and the hero actions wrap without overlap.

The earlier repeated office-card list and tall generic record forms are no longer the Masterbook entry point. Category pages use a separate visual record layout, while the new Atlas creator starts with name, type, and one important fact before hiding advanced fields behind an optional section.

The visual direction remains intentionally game-codex and field-guide inspired rather than copying a third-party app. An original arcane Atlas emblem has been reserved for the hero asset and is referenced by the app. The category system, local data, and wording remain original to The Ren Protocol.

## Atlas Expansion Check

Desktop and 375-pixel phone captures confirm that the original category emblems are visible as compact illustrated icons on every Atlas destination. They support, rather than replace, the plain-language labels. The Masterbook hero and category shelf remain readable with the new assets, and the two-column phone category grid retains large touch targets.

The Connections workspace now places the browser-local Relationship Web directly below its local metrics. Its empty state explains that it displays only relationships deliberately saved by the author and that it never guesses, sends, shares, or creates canon. On phone, the web's Add a bond action has a full-width tap target and the relationship area stacks cleanly without horizontal overflow. The Place dossier follows the same local-only Masterbook boundary and opens from a place folio rather than a repeated generic card.
