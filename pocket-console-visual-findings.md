# Pocket Story Console Visual Verification

The revised phone Desk uses a compact dark top bar, a fixed five-destination dock, and a clear More control. The primary writing action, current chapter word count, World, Plan, and Vault actions are visible without the repeated large navigation block from the previous mobile interface.

The 375-pixel review confirmed that the chapter word-count badge remains inside the Pocket Desk hero after adding its positioning anchor. The dock now contains exactly Desk, Write, World, Plan, and Vault; extra tools have moved to the top More control rather than creating a sixth cramped dock item.

The 1280-pixel desktop review confirmed that the same Pocket Desk expands into a concise three-action workspace beside the existing desktop author rail. It does not replace or alter the underlying local chapters, Masterbook records, Vault material, stats, exports, or private group workspace.
